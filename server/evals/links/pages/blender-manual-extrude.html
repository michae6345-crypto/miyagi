Extrude Region¶

Reference

Mode:

Edit Mode

Menu:

Mesh ‣ Extrude ‣ Extrude Region

Shortcut:

E

Extrude Region pulls the selected faces out of the mesh and creates new faces along the sides, so you can grow new shapes out of existing ones: a chimney out of a roof, a handle out of a cup.

Usage¶

Try it on the default cube:

  1. Select the cube and press Tab to enter Edit Mode.

  2. Press 3 to switch to Face select mode.

  3. Click the top face of the cube to select it.

  4. Press E and move the mouse up to pull the face out.

  5. Click to confirm, or type 1 and press Return to extrude exactly one unit.

The new faces stay selected, so you can extrude again straight away to build up a shape step by step.

Options¶

After extruding, the Adjust Last Operation panel in the corner of the 3D Viewport shows the options:

Orientation

The space the movement is measured in: Global, Local or Normal.

Flip Normals

Turns the new faces inside out; rarely what you want.

Dissolve Orthogonal Edges

Removes edges that would only make a flat face more complicated.

Making a chimney¶

  1. Select a small face on the roof of a house model.

  2. Press I to inset the face so the chimney is narrower than the roof.

  3. Press E, then Z to lock the movement to the vertical axis.

  4. Move the mouse up and click when the chimney is tall enough.
