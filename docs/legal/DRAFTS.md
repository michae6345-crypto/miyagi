### The short version

- miyagi looks at your screen and code only during a lesson, to follow your step.

- miyagi is for people 13 and older.

### 1. What miyagi collects and why

**Screen (during lessons).** While a lesson is running, miyagi takes screenshots of your screen: a reduced full-screen image and a full-resolution crop around your cursor. It also reads text on screen (with on-device text recognition and the operating system's accessibility features) and the name of the frontmost app. Why: to see which step you are on and to check your work. Text read from the screen is scrubbed of things that look like email addresses, US Social Security numbers, phone numbers and card numbers before it is sent. **Screenshot images are sent as captured; they are not blurred or cropped to hide sensitive content.** Do not keep passwords, keys, private messages or other people's information on screen during a lesson. `[VERIFY: where the 13+ gate and "lessons only" capture are enforced; confirm capture never runs outside a lesson]`

**Code and editor (during lessons).** Through the VS Code companion, miyagi reads the file you are working on, your cursor and selection, and terminal output related to the lesson. Why: to check your code and explain errors. `[VERIFY: exact editor fields the companion sends, from vscode-ext source]`

**Lessons and tutorials.** When you pick a tutorial, miyagi uses the video link and public information about the video (title, description, chapters, captions) to build lesson steps. Why: to turn the tutorial into steps and checks. `[VERIFY: final YouTube data path after RISKS.md risk 3 changes]`

**Account (optional).** If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account identifier through our sign-in provider, Supabase. Why: to identify you `[VERIFY: what the account is used for today; the app works without one]`. Your session tokens are stored only on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's keychain.

**Install token.** On first run the app gets a random install token from miyagi-cloud and sends basic device information (app name, operating system, processor type). Why: so miyagi-cloud can apply usage limits and stop abuse without knowing who you are. `[VERIFY: what miyagi-cloud stores with the token: IP address, timestamps, request counts]`

**Your own API keys (optional).** If you enter your own AI provider key, it is stored on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's keychain, and sent only to that provider.

**Crash reports and usage (off by default).** If you turn on "Share crash reports and usage" in Settings > Privacy, miyagi sends error messages with personal paths and keys scrubbed, and counts such as how many lessons you started. Never your code or screen. `[VERIFY: where these go (which service), and what is scrubbed; telemetry.ts is not on the integration branch]`

**Website.** See the cookie notice below. The site has no analytics and sets no tracking cookies.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive information such as health or financial data. If it appears on your screen during a lesson, it may be included in a screenshot (see above).

**Cookies and storage on miyagi.study**
Effective `[EFFECTIVE DATE]`

- **Download code.** When you type the download code, your browser remembers it in local storage (key `miyagi-code`) so you do not have to type it again. It never leaves your browser. Clear it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

When it loads, YouTube may store identifiers in your browser and receive your IP address, under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). `[After RISKS.md risk 15 fix:]` The video loads only when you click play.
- **Video thumbnails** load from i.ytimg.com (YouTube), and some page code loads from cdn.jsdelivr.net. Those services see your IP address, like any web request. `[VERIFY: still true after any self-hosting change]`
- **Hosting.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address for security (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The site does not set any cookies of its own. Because nothing on it tracks you, there is no cookie banner. Questions: `[CONTACT EMAIL]`.
