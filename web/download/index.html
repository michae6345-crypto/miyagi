Questions

Is it safe to open?

Yes. Your computer warns you because miyagi isn't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet. That's on our list.

On a Mac, miyagi asks for two permissions, Screen Recording and Accessibility, and may ask once to control VS Code so it can open the right file. You can switch any of them off in System Settings at any time. It never uses your camera or microphone.

To check your file is the real one, compare its SHA-256:

What does it read on my screen?

During a lesson, miyagi looks at your screen to see which step you're on, read your code and its output, and point at the right button.

Screenshots go straight from your computer to the AI provider, Anthropic, only to check your step. There's no miyagi server in between.

Screenshots go to the AI model only to check your step. Our server passes them along and doesn't keep them.

Does it cost money?

miyagi itself is free. For now, setup asks for your own Anthropic API key, and Anthropic bills its use to your account. A Gemini key is optional; add one to learn from video tutorials.

You can change either key later in Settings.

No. miyagi is free right now. You don't need an account or an API key.

If you'd rather use your own Anthropic key, you can add it in Settings.

Which AI models does it use?

Claude by Anthropic builds your lesson, checks each step and answers your questions. Gemini by Google watches video tutorials so miyagi knows what each step shows.

What do I need?

Mac: Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 13 or later. To check: Apple menu > About This Mac > Chip.

Windows: 64-bit, Windows 10 or 11.

miyagi teaches inside VS Code. Setup connects it for you, or links to it if you don't have it yet.

How do I uninstall it?

Mac: quit miyagi, then drag it from Applications to the Trash. To remove its settings too, delete ~/Library/Application Support/miyagi and, in Keychain Access, the item miyagi Safe Storage. The miyagi folder in your home folder holds miyagi's engine and your lessons; it stays until you delete it.

Windows: Settings > Apps > Installed apps > miyagi > Uninstall.

Privacy. miyagi doesn't sell your data or show ads, and screenshots are used only to check your step. This page has no cookies and no trackers.
